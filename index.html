02 / OUR STORY

Rooted in Ahmedabad.
Made to be read.

Hardik Offset is a printing press based in Old Lathi Bazar, Ahmedabad, known in public records for its association with newspaper and periodical printing.

Our story is tied to printed publications in Gujarati, Hindi, Marathi and English — a reminder that thoughtful printing helps ideas reach people, across languages and communities.

Government press records associate Raju Hasaram Dhanrajani with Hardik Offset. He is also named as the owner, publisher and editor of the Gujarati weekly Kalpatru.
